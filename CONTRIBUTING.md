# Contributing

Open an issue before substantial architectural changes. Keep changes focused and include relevant offline checks. Do not introduce automatic inference, provider/billing fallback or authentication-token import. Follow `AGENTS.md` and `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`.

Use synthetic images/text for tests and screenshots. Never attach login callbacks, tokens, saved profiles, corporate screenshots or personal account data to public issues. Preserve third-party license notices and the separate ownership of provider accounts.
